# 🚀 Getting started with ValuSafe

Welcome! This short guide helps you set up your inventory in a few minutes.

*As of version 4.2.1, July 2026*

---

## Step 1 — Create rooms and locations

Rooms, locations and positions describe **where** your valuables are — in three levels: room → location → position.

Go to **Settings → Master Data → Manage Locations** and create your first rooms, for example:

- Living room
- Bedroom
- Basement
- Office

Within a room you can add more precise locations and positions if you need them (e.g. "Cupboard left" within "Living room").

---

## Step 2 — Create categories

Categories describe **what** you own.

Go to **Settings → Master Data → Manage Categories** and create your first categories, for example:

- Electronics
- Jewellery
- Furniture
- Musical instruments

---

## Step 3 — Add your first item

Click the **＋ button** at the bottom right or **+ New** in the toolbar at the top.

Fill in at least:
- **Name** — what the item is called
- **Category** — from step 2
- **Room / location** — from step 1
- **Purchase price** — for the insurance valuation

Optionally you can add a photo, the purchase date and notes.

---

## Step 4 — Set up backups

To keep your data safe, set up regular backups.

Go to **Admin backend → Backup management** and create your first backup.

Recommendation: switch on the automatic cron backup (daily).

---

## Step 5 — Switch on two-factor authentication (recommended)

For extra protection of your account: go to **Settings → Manage two-factor**, scan the QR code shown with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator) and confirm the 6-digit code. Keep the backup codes shown afterwards in a safe place.

---

## Everyday tips

- **Search & filter** — Open the search bar at the top to filter by name, category, location or price
- **Barcode scan** — When adding an item you can scan its barcode (books, CDs, vinyl records, groceries, products)
- **Documents** — You can store invoices and warranty certificates directly with the item
- **Change the order** — In the list view you can freely rearrange your items by drag & drop
- **Export** — Under **Export** you can output your inventory as PDF or CSV — handy for your insurer
